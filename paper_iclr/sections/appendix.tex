\subsection{Attractor diagnosis}
\label{app:attractor}
Diversity metrics under sustained modulation: 0.006 (always-on) vs.\ 0.079
(prefill-skip) vs.\ 0.108 (turn-gate), against a 0.099 no-modulation ceiling. Onset at
turns 2--3. Adjacent-round similarity across 100 benchmark sessions: 0.094 --- close to
ceiling, confirming the five-level pressure benchmark (Section~\ref{sec:dissociation})
is not contaminated by this effect within its round budget. See
Section~\ref{sec:limitations} for the open multi-turn deployment question this leaves.

\subsection{Probe decodability}
\label{app:probe}
The linear-probe result reported in Section~\ref{sec:why-channels-differ} (decodable
when modulation is active, 22\% when zeroed) is repeated here to make explicit that it
fails a strict implicit-memory criterion: a sufficiently well-positioned probe with
access to internal activations can recover the conditioned belief above chance even
though it never appears in the model's own reasoning trace or surfaces to the
interlocutor. This is not in tension with Section~\ref{sec:why-channels-differ}'s
claim --- decodable-by-probe and read-by-the-model-as-an-assertion are different
properties, and we report both readings of the same number rather than picking the one
that is more flattering to either argument.

\subsection{The auxiliary training signal ($d^*$)}
\label{app:dstar}
An early version of the write function's training objective included an auxiliary
steering-direction loss, $d^*$, intended as a regularizer. A reconstruction audit
established that $d^*$ was not in fact applied at generation time in the dispositional
evaluation reported in the main body. The ablation reported in
Section~\ref{sec:isolation-rank} (dose sweep and $d^*$-only condition) confirms it is
not load-bearing for the persistence result: applying it at inference time does not help
and, at higher doses, hurts, and a condition built entirely from $d^*$ collapses to the
same regime as CAA steering. We disclose it here because the released training code
still contains the auxiliary loss term, and the paper and the code must not diverge on
what does and does not matter.
